Different Memories

A mobile game about the things everyone remembers, and remembers wrong.

What it is

The Mandela Effect is the name for a memory that large numbers of people share and that turns out not to match the record: a spelling, a logo, a line from a film. Different Memories turns those into a game.

You play a round of 20 questions. Some are multiple choice, some are true or false, and some ask you to type the answer from memory. After each one the app shows what is actually true, a short explanation, and how everyone else answered the same question, so you can see whether it was just you.

At the end of the round you get a result based on how your memories lined up, with a card you can keep coming back to as you play more rounds.
