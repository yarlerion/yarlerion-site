Privacy Policy

What Yarlerion LLC collects, and what it does not, on this website and in the Different Memories app.

Effective 11 November 2026

Who we are

Yarlerion LLC ("Yarlerion", "we") is a company based in Kentucky, USA. This policy covers the website at yarlerion.com and the Different Memories mobile app. ARPG Central has its own website and is not covered by this page.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • The Different Memories app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not show advertising, use advertising trackers or sell information about anyone.
  • The only information the app sends us is an anonymous count of right and wrong answers per question.

This website

This website is a set of plain pages. We do not set cookies and we do not run analytics or tracking scripts on it.

The site is hosted by GitHub. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details of a visit, such as IP address and browser type, in order to deliver and protect the site. We do not receive that information in a form that identifies you.

The Different Memories app

Kept on your device. Your progress, meaning which questions you have seen and whether you answered them correctly, is saved on your phone so the app can avoid repeating questions and work out your result. It is not sent to us, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Sent to us. When you answer a question, the app adds one to a shared total of right or wrong answers for that question. This is how the app shows how everyone else answered. The total is not linked to you: the app does not send a name, an account, a device identifier or your answer history with it. Questions you skip are not counted.

Our service provider. The shared totals are stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). When the app contacts that service, Google processes technical information needed to deliver and secure it, such as IP address. We do not use that information to identify anyone.

What the app does not do. It does not use analytics, advertising identifiers, location, the camera, the microphone, photos or contacts.

Planned changes

Different Memories is still in development. Before public release we plan to let players choose an age range, so the app can show how different age groups remember the same things. We would ask for a range only, never a date of birth, with an option not to answer, and it would be counted anonymously in the same way as answers. This policy will be updated before that feature, or any advertising or in-app purchase, is added.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply and to deal with your request, and we do not add it to a mailing list.

Children

This website and the Different Memories app are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Because we do not hold information that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. If you have emailed us and would like that correspondence removed, or you have any question about this policy, write to us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
